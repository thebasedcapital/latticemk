## Honest negatives

The raw Q4_K_M quality is better than v2 GPTQ (12.1664 vs 12.4435 PPL), so it is **not quality-matched** under the strict no-worse-PPL criterion. Q4_0 at 12.9617 is the closest weaker-quality llama.cpp baseline, 4.17% worse than v2 GPTQ and within the explicitly chosen 5% comparison band `[derived, bench/lm11/ppl.json; 5% band assumed]`. RTN v2 at 14.6557 is not matched to either one.

llama.cpp Q4_0 KV on Q4_K_M weights destroys quality: PPL 12.1664 → 49.3823. Lower KV bytes alone cannot justify a speed claim against GPTQ-v2 at PPL 12.4435 `[measured, bench/lm11/ppl.json]`.
