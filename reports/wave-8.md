## 4. Honest negatives

- **The 1.5x target was missed at every context.** The GEMM (+0.85 ms at M=4) is untouched; tensor cores do not help
  it on Turing (fragment preparation costs 275 instructions per 16 MMA ops).
- **Batch mode scales poorly at long context** (3.28x at 2048): each sequence has its own cache, so KV bytes grow
  with M. Causal mode shares one cache and scales better.
- **mt3 regresses in places:** batch ctx 2048 M=3 and M=5 vs mt2; LM-20's causal M=1 at 8192 is slightly slower.
- **No end-to-end speculative decoding yet:** break-even thresholds are derived; acceptance rates are unmeasured.
- LM-24's masked HF reference covers 384 tokens and two evictions, not the full 16k session; it uses its own bitwise
  regression because its init ABI adds `kv_len`.
- Generated schedule JSONs from this wave are not committed (153.7 MB, 23 duplicates); manifests record hash and
  verdict.
